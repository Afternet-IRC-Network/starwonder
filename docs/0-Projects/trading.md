# todo plan for trading


keep things simple by not iterating every damn item type, instead brush broad categories

thing / expensive version of thing

eg: 
metals / precious metals
minerals / rare minerals
food / luxury food
clothing / designer apparel
livestock / advanced breed livestock
equipment / high tech equipment
medical supplies / high tech medical supplies
electronics / high tech electronics

then these can be ranked on a scale that basically makes core (near earth) cheaper to buy goods that are up the supply chain (ie, electronics, high tech items), and rim (far from earth) cheaper to buy raw materials (minerals, metals, regular food etc) lets call it technological complexity

then we take that as a base and overlay a fractal-like seed based field over it to adjust it a bit (not too much)

then we do some kind of simple accounting on each station for what they have and make them spend more for things they dont have, and sell cheaper for things they have a lot of. we'll need to do some careful design on the balancing of the market with all of this to make it fun and fair.
